Cancellation and Refund Policy


1. All refund requests must be submitted in writing to info@apscvir-2026.com and will be charged a cancellation processing fee of $50.

2. Cancellations made before March 31, 2026 will receive a full refund.

2. Cancellations made before May 20, 2026 will receive a 50% refund of the registration fee.

3. No refunds will be issued on and after May 20, 2026. Refunds will not be given for failure to attend, late arrival, unattended events, or early departure.

4. If you are unable to attend, a substituent is welcome at no additional charge. The original attendee must provide a letter of authorization along with a cancellation letter before May 20, 2026. The substituent must also register online in advance.
