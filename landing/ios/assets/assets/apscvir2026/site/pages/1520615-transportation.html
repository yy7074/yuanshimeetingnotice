📍 Venue: Hall A1, Suzhou International Expo Center

🏠 Address: No. 688 East Suzhou Avenue, Suzhou Industrial Park, Suzhou

🚇 Metro: Take Metro Line 1 to Culture & Expo Center Station and leave via Exit 3.

🚗 Self-Travel Guidance

✈️ From Shanghai Hongqiao Airport

Option 1: High-speed rail (Recommended / Fastest)

  • Airport → Hongqiao Railway Station: Walk or Metro Line 2/10 (≈20 min)
  • Train to Suzhou Industrial Park Station / Suzhou Station / Suzhou North Station (≈25–35 min)

Option 2: Airport bus

  • Take the Hongqiao Airport–Suzhou bus and get off at Suzhou Industrial Park (International Building).

  • Take a taxi for about RMB 15, or walk to Central Park Station on Metro Line 1 and travel one stop to Culture & Expo Center Station.
  • Duration: 1.5h | Fare: ¥50

Option 3: Taxi / Driving

  • Distance: 80 km | Time: 1–1.5h | Cost: ¥300–350

✈️ From Shanghai Pudong Airport

Option 1: Metro + High-speed rail (Recommended)

  • Metro Line 2 (toward Xujing East) to Hongqiao Railway Station (≈1.5h)
  • High-speed rail to Suzhou (25–35 min)

Option 2: Airport bus

  • Take the Pudong Airport–Suzhou bus and get off at Suzhou Industrial Park (International Building).

  • Transfer: Taxi about ¥15, or walk to Central Park Station (Metro Line 1).

  • Duration: 2.5h | Fare: ¥80

Option 3: Taxi / Driving

  • Distance: 130–150 km | Time: ≈2h | Cost: ¥450–500

🚆 From Suzhou Stations to Suzhou International Expo Center

Suzhou North Station

  • Metro: Line 2 (toward Sangtiandao) → Guangjinan Road (First: 05:44, Last: 23:44) → Line 1 (toward Zhongnanjie) to Culture & Expo Center Station, Exit 3 (First: 05:44, Last: 23:44) (50 min, ¥5)
  • Taxi: 17 km, 30 min, ¥45–50

Suzhou Station

  • Metro: Line 4 (toward Tongli) → Leqiao Station (First: 05:43, Last: 22:39) → Line 1 (toward Zhongnanjie) to Culture & Expo Center Station, Exit 3 (First: 06:03, Last: 22:58) (35 min, ¥4)
  • Bus: Express No.9, North Square of Suzhou Station (50 min, ¥2)
  • Taxi: 11 km, 20 min, ¥25–30

Suzhou Industrial Park Station

  • Taxi: 3–4 km, 10 min, ¥10–15 (Fastest)
  • Metro: Line 8 (toward Chefang) → Times Square  (First: 06:15, Last: 22:55) → Line 1 (toward Mudu) to Culture & Expo Center Station, Exit 3 (First: 06:01, Last: 22:42)

 Parking

  • There are about 3000 parking spaces around the venue.
  • Suzhou Expo offers free parking for four hours. And five yuan will be paid for each 30 minutes beyond the four hours. Less than 30 minutes will be deemed as 30 minutes for the parking.
  • The highest parking cost per car per day each time will be ￥100.
  • Cash, alipay, WeChat or bank cards are acceptable for the payment.

💡 Tip: We recommend using Amap or Baidu Maps for navigation in mainland China.
