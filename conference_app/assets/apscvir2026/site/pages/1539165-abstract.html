Key Dates of Late-Breaking Abstract

Submission opens: 30 March 2026

Submission closes: 12 April 2026

Notification of results: 26 April 2026

Late-Breaking Abstracts are intended for high-impact, practice-changing research that could not be submitted by the regular abstract deadline due to the timing of data analysis or study completion. If you have important new findings that advance the field of cardiovascular and interventional radiology, we warmly encourage you to submit.


Submission Guidelines



1. Submitted data must be original, unpublished, and not presented at any other meeting prior to APSCVIR 2026.

2. Studies should report final results or key interim analyses that became available only after the regular abstract submission deadline.

3. All submissions will undergo peer review. Acceptance will be highly competitive due to the limited number of presentation slots.

* Abstracts must be submitted by the submission deadline, which is 12 April 2026 (23:59 GMT+8).

Submission Guidelines


1. The abstract words limit is 800 with a minimum requirement of 100 words (excluding title, author information, and affiliations).
2. All abstract submissions are required to contain the following information for consideration and review:
● Purpose
● Materials and methods
● Results
● Conclusion
● Table and Figures (Optional)
● References (Optional)
3. Abstracts must be submitted by the submission deadline, which is 31 December 2025, (23:59 GMT+8).
