About APSCVIR

The Asia Pacific Society of Cardiovascular and Interventional Radiology (APSCVIR) is an international organization that brings together interventional radiologists from the region, working to improve the level of clinical practice, education and research in the field of interventional radiology in the Asia Pacific Region. The society is a collaborative effort of 16 organizational members.

APSCVIR meets annually to encourage international exchange of knowledge vital to Cardiovascular & Interventional Radiology among medical doctors in different territories of the Asia Pacific region.

Objective of Society

Meet regularly to encourage international exchange of knowledge vital to cardiovascular and interventional radiology among radiologists, interventional radiologists and individuals in related fields in different countries of the Asia Pacific region.
