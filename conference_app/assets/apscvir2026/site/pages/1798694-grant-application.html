To support academic exchange and encourage participation of early-career interventional radiologists, APSCVIR 2026 offers travel grants and abstract awards in collaboration with international partner organizations.

AKAMS Travel Grants

Supported by the Asia–Korea–Africa Medical Scholarship Association (AKAMS), APSCVIR 2026 will provide travel grants to support eligible participants from the Asia-Pacific region.

Grant Details

  • Number of awards: 10
  • Grant amount: USD 500 per awardee
  • Purpose: Travel support for participation in APSCVIR 2026
  • Meeting dates and venue: June 11–14, 2026, Suzhou, China

Eligibility

Applicants must meet all of the following criteria:

  • International residents, fellows, or early-career interventional radiologists (≤ 35 years; recommendation letter from the head of department required)
  • Based in the Asia-Pacific region
  • Presenting authors of abstracts accepted for APSCVIR 2026

Application Process

Eligible applicants should submit an application by email to:

  • Email: info@apscvir-2026.com

The application should include:

  • Full name
  • Institution
  • Accepted abstract title and abstract ID
  • Brief curriculum vitae
  • Recommendation letter from the head of department
  • Passport photo page
  • Deadline for application: January 31, 2026

Award Notification

  • AKAMS Travel Grant recipients will be notified by email prior to the APSCVIR 2026 meeting.

ISMIO Abstract Awards

The International Society of Multidisciplinary Interventional Oncology (ISMIO) will provide abstract awards to support presenters at APSCVIR 2026, recognizing excellence in IR research and academic contribution.

Award Categories

  • ISMIO Oral Abstract Awards - Top 10 oral abstracts, USD 300 per award
  • ISMIO Poster Abstract Awards - Top 10 poster abstracts, USD 200 per award

Award Announcement

  • ISMIO award recipients will be announced during the Gala Dinner at APSCVIR 2026.

Important Note

  • ISMIO abstract awards are reserved for presenters who are not recipients of the AKAMS travel grant.
  • The awardees are selected by the Scientific Committee and are independent of nationality.
  • Invited speakers are not eligible for Abstract Awards.
